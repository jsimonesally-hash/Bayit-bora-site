House Rule 001

Bayit Bora: Preserving Culture, Empowering Creators

Art has always been humanity's most sacred mechanism—a vehicle for prophecy, an instructor of wisdom, a bearer of history, and a spark for inspiration. Yet, modern algorithmic landscapes systematically marginalize voices categorized as micro or macro threats, controlling visibility where influence has become the primary currency. Bayit Bora exists to dismantle these structural gatekeepers, constructing a permanent, equitable ecosystem where authentic cultural narratives flourish without suppression.

By combining decentralized architecture with physical craftsmanship, Bayit Bora bridges the digital and tactile worlds, serving as an immutable archive dedicated to safeguarding cultural heritage against erasure and algorithmic censorship.

Our Architectural Framework

  • Tangible Stewardship: A strictly capped collection of 100,000 foundational NFTs, each intrinsically linked to physical artwork and tangible artifacts, ensuring digital ownership translates into real-world presence.
  • Epoch-Bound Utility: An issuance of 200,000 dynamic micro-tokens that expire per holder, designed to cultivate active participation, circulate influence, and prevent speculative stagnation.
  • Algorithmic Equity: Proprietary smart contract mechanics engineered to distribute reach and revenue fairly, ensuring artists designated as narrative risks are compensated directly rather than suppressed by centralized recommendation engines.
  • Community DAO Fund: Creators retain the majority of sales, while the remaining balance feeds a collective fund managed by the community to prioritize high-impact cultural preservation.

Our proprietary smart contracts are currently in active development to ensure that influence serves the creators of culture, not the platforms that host them.
